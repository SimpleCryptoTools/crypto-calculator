Crypto Profit Calculator Guide

Learn how to calculate cryptocurrency profit, loss, and return on investment with easy examples.

How Do You Calculate Crypto Profit?

Crypto profit is the difference between the amount you receive when selling cryptocurrency and the amount you originally paid to buy it. Trading fees can affect your final result.

Profit or Loss = Selling Value − Buying Cost − Fees

Example: Calculating Bitcoin Profit

Imagine you buy cryptocurrency for $1,000 and later sell it for $1,200. Your trading fees total $10.

  • Buying cost: $1,000
  • Selling value: $1,200
  • Total fees: $10
  • Net profit: $190

Your return after fees is 19% of your original $1,000 investment.

How to Calculate Crypto Loss

If you buy an asset for $1,000 and sell it for $800, you lose $200 before fees. Include transaction fees to estimate your complete result.

Why Trading Fees Matter

Exchanges may charge fees when you buy or sell cryptocurrency. Network fees may also apply when transferring assets. Ignoring these costs can make your estimated profit higher than your actual profit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is crypto profit calculated before or after fees?

For a more realistic result, subtract applicable trading and transaction fees from your profit.

What is crypto return on investment?

Return on investment (ROI) expresses profit or loss as a percentage of the original investment.

Does a profit calculator predict future prices?

No. A calculator estimates results from the values you enter. It cannot predict future cryptocurrency prices.
